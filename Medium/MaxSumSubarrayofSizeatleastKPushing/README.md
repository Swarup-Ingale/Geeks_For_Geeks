<h2><a href="#">Max Sum Subarray of Size at least KPushing...</a></h2>
<h3>Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given an array <strong>arr[] </strong>and an integer <strong>k</strong>, find the maximum sum among all contiguous subarrays having a length greater than or equal to k.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [1, -2, 2, -3], k = 3
<strong>Output: </strong>1
<strong>Explanation: </strong>The sub-array of length at least 3 </span><span style="font-size: 18px;">that produces greatest sum is [1, -2, 2]</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong><strong> </strong>arr[] = [1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1], k = 2
<strong>Output: </strong>6<br><strong>Explanation: </strong></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">The sub-array of length at least 2 </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">that produces greatest sum is [1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1]</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong><strong> </strong>arr[] = [-4, -2, 1, -3], k = 2
<strong>Output: </strong>-1<br><strong>Explanation: </strong></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">The sub-array of length at least 2 </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">that produces greatest sum is [-2, 1]</span></pre>
