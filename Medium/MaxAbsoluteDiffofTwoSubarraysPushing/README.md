<h2><a href="#">Max Absolute Diff of Two SubarraysPushing...</a></h2>
<h3>Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given an array of integers <strong>arr[], </strong>find two non-overlapping contiguous sub-arrays such that the absolute difference between the sum of two sub-arrays is maximum.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples :</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [-2, -3, 4, -1, -2, 1, 5, -3]
<strong>Output:</strong> 12
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Two subarrays are [-2, -3] and [4, -1, -2, 1, 5]</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [2, -1, -2, 1, -4, 2, 8]
<strong>Output:</strong> 16
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Two subarrays are [-1, -2, 1, -4] and [2, 8] </span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>2 ≤ arr.size() ≤ 10<sup>5</sup><br>-10<sup>3</sup> ≤ arr[i] ≤ 10<sup>3</sup></span></p>
