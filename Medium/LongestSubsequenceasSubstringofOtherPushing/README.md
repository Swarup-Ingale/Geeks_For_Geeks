<h2><a href="#">Longest Subsequence as Substring of OtherPushing...</a></h2>
<h3>Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given two uppercase strings <strong>a</strong> and <strong>b</strong>, find the length of the longest subsequence of a that appears as a substring of b.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong> </span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Input: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">a =</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;"> </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">"ABCD",</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;"> </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">b =</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;"> </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">"BACDBDCD"
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Output: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">3
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Explanation: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">"ACD" is a subsequence of a and also appears as a substring of b. Hence, the answer is 3.<br></span></span></pre>
<pre><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Input: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">a =</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;"> </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">"ABC",</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;"> </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">b =</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;"> </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">"BAC"
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Output: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">2
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Explanation: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">"AC" is a subsequence of a and also appears as a substring of b. Hence, the answer is 2.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1 ≤ |a|, |b| ≤ 10<sup>3</sup></span></p>
